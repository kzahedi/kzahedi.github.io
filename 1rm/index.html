1RM Planner

Plan the weeks before, then run max day: a build-up from the empty bar to today’s target, the plates for every set, a rest clock, and your attempts. Every target comes from you; the app never suggests one.

Join the beta See how it works

Max day, set by set

Enter your last best and today’s target. The app works out the build-up ladder, tells you which plates come off and which go on, and starts the rest clock once the bar is loaded. Beeps count you in.

After a made attempt you enter the next weight yourself; the app shows the plates to change and asks once more if it’s well above your previous best. Miss, and you retry or call it a day. Your best goes straight into your history.

Plan the weeks before

A peaking block of four to eight weeks for one lift, two or three days a week. Every session lists the weights and the plates per side.

Log what you lifted. If a heavy set falls short, the rest of the block gets lighter; if two heavy days in a row go well above plan, it gets heavier. The last day hands over to max day.

See where you stand

Your tested maxes, your best so far, and an estimate from the sets you log, averaged over four weeks so one good day doesn’t fool you. Export everything as a CSV file whenever you like.

Your build-up, in your words

Type your scheme the way you’d tell a training partner:

bar ×5, 40 ×3, 60 ×2, 80 ×1 | rest 80: 2 min

Or just describe it. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence the app reads plain English or German on the phone itself. You see the session it would build before anything changes.

Stays on your phone

No account, no tracking, no ads. Your lifts are stored on your iPhone and nowhere else. Read the privacy policy.
