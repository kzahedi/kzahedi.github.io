Safety

The app shows this notice before first use, and you accept it to continue:

1RM Planner is a training aid, not medical advice and not a substitute for a doctor or a coach.

  • Lifting near your maximum can cause serious injury.
  • Warm up properly before you start. Use safe technique, collars, and where possible a spotter or safety arms.
  • You’re responsible for your equipment and your technique: check the bar, the collars and the rack before every heavy set.
  • The app never suggests a target weight. All targets come from you; the weights it calculates for your build-up are estimates. You decide what you lift.
  • Stop at once if you feel pain, dizziness or shortness of breath.
  • See a doctor before heavy training if you have heart or circulation problems, high blood pressure, lung problems, problems with your spine or joints, a recent injury or surgery, or if you are pregnant.
  • 1RM Planner is meant for adults aged 18 or older.

If you miss a build-up set three times, the app suggests stopping for the day. A bad day happens; come back fresh.

Common questions

Which iPhones does it run on?

Any iPhone with iOS 27. Describing your build-up in free text needs Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro or newer, switched on in Settings). The short notation works everywhere.

The plates look wrong for my gym.

Set your bar, collars and the number of plate pairs under Settings. The app only plans weights it can load with what you have.

I didn’t hear the rest beep.

Check Settings → Rest sounds. With the phone locked, the app uses a notification instead, so allow notifications for 1RM Planner in the iPhone’s Settings.

How do I get my data out?

On History, use the share button to export everything as a CSV file.

Is there an Android version?

It’s planned after the iPhone beta.
