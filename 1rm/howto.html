How it works

1RM Coach runs the part of max day between your warm-up and your last attempt. Warm up the way you always do first: mobility, an easy bar, whatever your routine is. Then open the app.

Your first max day

  1. On Attempt, pick the lift and enter your previous best and today’s target.
  2. Check the plan. It lists every build-up set from the empty bar to the attempt, with the plates per side, and the plates to bring to the bar.
  3. Tap Start session and lift the first set. Tap Done.
  4. The load screen shows which plates come off (red) and which go on (green). Tap Loaded when the bar is ready.
  5. The rest clock starts. Below it you see the next lift. Add 30 seconds or skip the rest if you’re ready early.
  6. Repeat until the attempt. Tap Made or Missed.
  7. After a make, the app proposes the next attempt; move it lighter or heavier, or finish. After a miss, retry or finish.

The session survives the app being closed. Open it again and you’re back where you left off. Turn the phone sideways on a bench and the clock sits on the left, the next lift on the right.

Plates

Set your bar, collars and how many pairs of each plate your gym has under Settings. The app only plans weights you can actually load, and it keeps plate changes small: most of the time plates only go on.

Rest and sounds

Each profile has rest times that grow with the weight. Under Settings → Rest sounds choose one beep at the end, a countdown from 3, or none. The beeps play even with the phone on silent and only lower your music. With the phone locked you get a notification instead.

Planning a block

On Plan, create a block: the lift, your current best, four to eight weeks, two or three days a week. Each session lists the sets with weights and plates. After training, open the session and log the reps you did.

If you miss reps on a set at 85 % or more, the rest of the block gets 2.5 % lighter. Two heavy days in a row well above plan make it 2.5 % heavier. The last day is the attempt: Start attempt takes you to max day with everything filled in.

History and the estimated max

The chart shows your tested maxes, your best so far, and an estimated max. The estimate comes from sets of 2 to 10 reps that you log yourself, using the Epley formula, with each training day’s best averaged over four weeks. Sets from a planned block don’t count towards it: they’re meant to be lighter than your limit and would pull the estimate down.

Describe your build-up in words

Under Settings → Lift profiles, open a profile and tap Describe in words. Write the build-up in a short notation:

bar×5, 40×3, 50×3, 60×2, 70×1, 80×1, 90×1 | target 1 | rest 0:60s, 80:2min, 90:3min

  • Sets are separated by commas: bar×5 is the empty bar, 40×3 is 40 % of your previous best for 3 reps.
  • 90×1×2 means two singles at 90 %.
  • target 1 sets the reps of the attempt.
  • rest 80:2min means 2 minutes of rest before every set at 80 % or more.
  • German works too: Stange ×5; 40 % ×3 | Ziel: 1 | Pause 80: 2 Minuten.

Or write it in your own words, in English or German, for example “5 at the bar, then triples at 40 and 50, singles from 70 up to 95”. Free text needs an iPhone with Apple Intelligence switched on and is read on the phone itself.

Tap Interpret. The preview shows the sets, the rest times and the session they give for an example best of 100 kg. Nothing changes until you tap Apply.
