Privacy

Last updated 3 October 2026.

1RM Coach does not collect any data about you. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code that reports anything.

What stays on your iPhone

Your lifts, sessions, logged sets, training blocks and settings are stored on your iPhone only. They are removed when you delete the app. Your iPhone’s own backups (iCloud or computer) may include them, as they do for any app, under your control.

Notifications

The rest alert is a local notification. It is scheduled on your iPhone and never sent through a server.

Describing your build-up in words

Short notation is read by the app itself. Free text is read by Apple’s on-device language model (Apple Intelligence). The app does not send your text to Apple’s servers, to Private Cloud Compute or to anyone else.

Export

The CSV export only leaves your iPhone when you share it yourself.

Beta testing

If you test the app through Apple’s TestFlight, Apple shares with the developer what TestFlight shares: crash reports, the feedback you send, and, if you were invited by email, your name and email address. They are used only to fix and improve the app.
