What the app is

1RM Planner helps barbell lifters plan training blocks and run a one-rep-max test: it calculates a build-up from the empty bar towards the target you enter, shows the plates to load, times the rests and records your results. It is free. It is a training aid, not medical advice, not a medical device and not a substitute for a doctor or a coach.

The app never suggests a target weight. Every target and every attempt weight comes from you. The weights it calculates for your build-up are estimates.

Your health and safety

  • Lifting near your maximum can cause serious injury.
  • See a doctor before heavy training if you have heart or circulation problems, high blood pressure, lung problems, problems with your spine or joints, a recent injury or surgery, or if you are pregnant.
  • Stop at once if you feel pain, dizziness or shortness of breath.
  • You are responsible for your equipment and your technique: check the bar, the collars and the rack before every heavy set, and where possible use a spotter or safety arms.
  • The app is meant for adults aged 18 or older.
